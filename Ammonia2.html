Ammonia2 — NH3 with Lone Pair

Each molecule is a nitrogen atom, three hydrogens, and one lone electron pair. Geometry is trigonal pyramidal with tetrahedral electron-domain arrangement (H-N-H ≈ 106.7°). Ammonia hydrogen bonds are modeled as directional N-H···LP interactions and are intentionally weaker/sparser than in water.
