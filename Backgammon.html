Backgammon

A little luck. A little strategy. Bring them home.

Computer · Walnut

How to play

You play ivory, moving 24 → 1. Walnut moves 1 → 24. Each player has 15 checkers.

  1. Roll the dice, choose a checker then a highlighted destination. A checker with only one legal destination moves immediately; Undo brings it back. Choose a die first if you want to use a particular number.
  2. A point with two or more opposing checkers is blocked. Landing on one sends it to the bar. Checkers on the bar must re-enter first. Your bar checker is selected automatically: click a highlighted entry point.
  3. Use both dice when possible, or the higher die if only one can be used. Doubles give four moves.
  4. Get every checker into your home board (ivory: 1–6, walnut: 19–24), then bear them off into the tray. First to bear off 15 wins.

Undo any of your moves before ending your turn. The opening roll gives each player one die; the higher roll starts using both dice. Single games scored in points. Either player may offer a double before rolling on their turn while the cube is centred. Accepting doubles the game value and gives the recipient sole rights to the next double. Passing ends the game at the previous value. A gammon scores twice the cube; a backgammon scores three times. No match play, automatic opening doubles, beavers or Jacoby rule. The computer uses a basic estimate for cube decisions.
