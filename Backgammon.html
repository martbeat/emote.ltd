Backgammon

A little luck. A little strategy. Bring them home.

You · Ivory
Computer · Walnut
How to play

You play ivory, moving 24 → 1. Walnut moves 1 → 24. Each player has 15 checkers.

  1. Roll the dice, choose a checker then a highlighted destination. Choose a die first if you want to use a particular number.
  2. A point with two or more opposing checkers is blocked. Landing on one sends it to the bar. Checkers on the bar must re-enter first.
  3. Use both dice when possible, or the higher die if only one can be used. Doubles give four moves.
  4. Get every checker into your home board (ivory: 1–6, walnut: 19–24), then bear them off into the tray. First to bear off 15 wins.

Undo any of your moves before ending your turn. The opening roll gives each player one die; the higher roll starts using both dice. Single games only in this draft, without the doubling cube.
