How to play CipherShift

  • Guess the hidden five-letter concept in six tries.
  • Green tiles are correct letters in the correct place.
  • Yellow tiles are letters in the word, but in the wrong place.
  • Grey tiles are not in the answer.
  • A blue inner outline means your guess also carries conceptual weight. It may share a theme, root, association or institutional meaning with the answer.
  • The Meaning Signal tells you whether your whole guess is conceptually close, even if the letters are not perfect.
  • Use the hint trail carefully. Hints unlock as you spend guesses or get semantically closer.
