About Current & nature notes

A solo companion to Flock & Field, inspired by UK rivers and the North Atlantic. Original vector illustrations simplify shape, markings and scale. Habitats represent supported populations and protected ranges, not small enclosures. Predators never remove another resident.

Talents are game abstractions. Nutrient cyclers include filter feeders, grazers and small plankton-feeding animals: they do not all clean water in the same way. Voyagers reward protecting connected waters, not a claim that all species make the same migration. Atlantic salmon return from the sea to spawn upriver; European eels travel out to the Sargasso Sea to breed. Read the Wildlife Trusts migration guide.

Optional water sounds are synthesised atmosphere, not animal recordings. Motion pauses outside the visible area and respects reduced-motion settings. Progress and best scores stay in this browser. On eMote, basic start and completion events record the game name and a broad duration group. No names or saved games are sent.

Welcome to Current

Every current carries possibility.

You have four seasons with six turns each. Reach your chosen points goal by the end of winter.

  1. Welcome visitors. Spend care to settle a species in its named habitat. Each species settles once and earns its arrival points.
  2. Make room. Each habitat starts with two spaces. Restore it for 4 care, then 6 care. Each restoration adds a space, 3 points and 1 care at each season end.
  3. Build a living network. Nutrient cyclers add care when protecting water. Hunters add points when observing. Shoalers add points when the food web thrives. When all three habitats are populated, voyagers add another point each to every survey. Voyagers also earn 1 point at each season end and a 4-point finale bonus.
  4. Choose your rhythm. Surveying earns points for every resident, populated habitat and hunter. A thriving food web earns 2 points per shoaler, plus 2 per nutrient cycler if you have a shoaler. These links represent a healthy food web, rather than a feeding simulation.
  5. Follow the seasons. Earn up to 6 points from each seasonal goal. Final bonuses reward species variety, voyagers, all three habitats and all four talents.

The waterscape stays safe: hunters never eat a resident. The voyager bonus rewards protecting their range across seasons. There is no timer or feeding bill. You can protect a species only once, in the habitat shown. These are conservation choices, rather than buying animals. Undo reverses your latest turn, including a season change.
