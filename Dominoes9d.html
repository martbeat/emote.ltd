Scoring Guide

Score when the sum of the exposed ends hits a multiple of 3 or 5. Doubles count on both ends.

  • End total 6 → score 2
  • End total 10 → score 2
  • End total 15 → score 8 (3 + 5)
  • End total 20 → score 4
