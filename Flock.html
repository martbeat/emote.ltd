    How to play · a minute in the field

    Build a sanctuary against the computer or a friend. Each player takes six turns per season, across four seasons. Choose one action each turn. A season ends automatically after both players finish their sixth turn: its bonus is added, the next goal becomes active and every bird’s once-per-season ability becomes ready again. Your sanctuary and resources carry over. After Winter’s bonus, the game ends. Highest score after Winter wins, with shared victory for a tie.

    1. Attract a bird: select a card in your hand, then use “Attract here” in one of its habitats. Pay the food cost shown. Each habitat has room for three birds. Birds arrive with one nest and the egg capacity printed on their card.
    2. Forage: gain 2 food, plus 1 per woodland bird. Food is a shared resource type and has no storage limit.
    3. Scout: choose a bird in the clearing, then Scout to take it. Also draw 1 random card per wetland bird, up to 2 extra. Your hand holds at most 8 cards. An empty deck is refilled with a new shuffled set.
    4. Lay eggs: distribute 2 eggs, plus 1 per meadow bird, automatically into available nests from woodland to wetland. If every nest is full, build a nest first.
    5. Build a nest: select a bird in your sanctuary. Spend 1 food to add a nest and space for 2 more eggs. Each bird can have three nests in total.
    6. Gather a flock: select a bird in your sanctuary and a card in your hand. Discard the hand card to give your chosen bird 2 flock points.

    Your engine: Forage activates woodland birds, Lay eggs activates meadow birds and Scout activates wetland birds. Each bird's ability triggers once per season, after the action. A filled dot means its ability is ready; an open dot means used. Extra eggs go on that bird if it has space. Extra cards respect the hand limit. Nest and flock actions do not activate habitats.

    Scoring: printed bird points + 1 per egg + 1 per flock point + seasonal bonuses. At each season's end, both players score their own goal: Spring 2 per extra nest built; Summer 1 per pair of eggs; Autumn 2 per occupied habitat; Winter 1 per pair of flock points. Eggs and flocks stay for final scoring. Food and cards left in hand are worth no points.

    Action buttons preview exact gains, bird abilities and capacity limits before you act. Turn effects explain your move beside your highlighted bird or habitat. Your sanctuary stays on screen. Afterwards, a clearly labelled Computer turn summary shows the opponent’s move; open Review computer move if you want its details. They wait for you: use Next effect and Back to follow the turn at your own pace, or Play slowly for automatic steps. Pause stops automatic playback. Use Skip turn or switch turn effects off for faster play. Coach offers optional suggestions without choosing for you. For a first turn, select the Robin in your hand and attract it to the woodland. Then try eggs or forage. Undo reverses your action and the computer's response together. In two-player mode, pass the screen after each turn; hands are visible when viewed.
