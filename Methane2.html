Methane2 — CH4 Tetrahedral Molecules

Each molecule is methane (CH₄): one carbon atom bonded to four hydrogens in a tetrahedral geometry (H-C-H ≈ 109.5°). Methane is nonpolar and does not hydrogen-bond; cohesion is modeled with short-range dispersion-style attraction and steric repulsion.
