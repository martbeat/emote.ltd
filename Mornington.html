Mornington Crescent: Procedural Nonsense Edition

A playable rules-light parody: you try to reach Mornington Crescent while the announcer claims the rules are ancient, binding, and very much in force.

Tip: choose a station, click Play Move. First to land on Mornington Crescent wins. If you select Mornington Crescent directly, the game will decide whether that is “allowed.”
