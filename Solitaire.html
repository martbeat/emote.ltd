How to play
  1. Build each foundation, top right, from ace to king in the same suit. Move all 52 cards there to win.
  2. In the seven columns, build down in alternating colours. A red six goes on a black seven. You can move a whole ordered run together. Only a king, or a run starting with a king, can fill an empty column.
  3. Select a face-up card, then tap a highlighted destination. Double-click a top card to send it to its foundation, or use To foundation. You can also drag cards with a mouse.
  4. Tap the stock to draw one or three cards. Only the top waste card is available. When the stock is empty, tap it to recycle the waste. There is no limit on passes.
  5. Covered cards turn over automatically when exposed. Undo any move, including drawing and recycling. Hints suggest legal moves, but do not guarantee a win. Random deals may be unwinnable.

Choose a draw mode for your next deal. Restart deals the same cards again. Finish game appears once all cards are exposed and the stock and waste are empty.
