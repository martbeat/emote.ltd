About Underleaf & nature notes

A solo companion to Flock & Field. Original vector illustrations highlight broad shapes, with simplified markings and scale. Optional sound sketches are synthesised for atmosphere and do not identify particular species. Motion respects your device’s reduced-motion setting and pauses for creatures outside the visible area. Habitat choices, encounter chances and talents are game abstractions. Residents represent supported populations, rather than individuals living through all four seasons. Hunters do not remove your other residents.

Spiders and harvestmen are arachnids. Woodlice are crustaceans. Worms, molluscs and myriapods have a place here too. Flower borders, dead wood and damp corners support different invertebrate communities. Learn more from the RHS wildlife gardening guide.

Your garden and best scores stay in this browser. On eMote, basic start and completion events record the game name and a broad duration group. No names or saved gardens are sent.

Welcome to Underleaf

Leave a little room for life.

You have four seasons with six turns each. Reach your chosen points goal by the end of winter.

  1. Welcome visitors. Spend care to settle a species in its named habitat. Each species settles once and earns its arrival points.
  2. Make room. Each habitat starts with two spaces. Restore it for 4 care, then 6 care. Each restoration adds a space, 3 points and 1 care at each season end.
  3. Build a living network. Recyclers add care when tending. Hunters add points when observing. Pollinators add points when blooming. Musicians earn a 4-point finale bonus.
  4. Choose your rhythm. Looking closer earns points for every resident, populated habitat and hunter. Blooming earns 2 points per pollinator, plus 2 per recycler if you have a pollinator. These links represent a healthy garden, not direct feeding.
  5. Follow the seasons. Earn up to 6 points from each seasonal goal. Final bonuses reward species variety, musicians, all three habitats and all four talents.

The garden stays safe: hunters never eat a resident. There is no timer or feeding bill. Undo reverses your latest turn, including a season change.
