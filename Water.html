H2O Phase Toy MD

Visual phase demo: Ice, Liquid, Steam. Flexible tri-atomic water (bond stretch + angle bend) so you can see momentum, rotation and vibration. Weak links show approximate hydrogen bonding (O···H) and/or O–O proximity. Includes a strong short-range electron-cloud repulsion term to avoid unrealistic close non-bonded overlap while bonded O–H sharing remains attractive via bond/angle terms.

This version adds a Berendsen-style barostat, a TIP4P/2005-like charge-site mode, and Ewald-screened Coulomb terms as a practical stepping-stone toward full PME. It is still a visual, reduced-unit approximation.

Tip
For ice-like solid behavior: lower temperature, reduce box size (higher density), and let it settle.
For steam: click Steam, increase box size and temperature, reduce LJ ε if you want less clumping.
