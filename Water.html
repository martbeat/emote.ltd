H2O Phase Toy MD

Visual phase demo: Ice, Liquid, Steam. Flexible tri-atomic water (bond stretch + angle bend) so you can see momentum, rotation and vibration. Weak links show approximate hydrogen bonding (O···H) and/or O–O proximity. Includes a strong short-range electron-cloud repulsion term to avoid unrealistic close non-bonded overlap while bonded O–H sharing remains attractive via bond/angle terms.

Thermo

For more “natural” motion, turn the thermostat off once a phase settles. Energy will drift (toy integrator).

Forces

Vibration and bending

Higher k means stiffer bonds (less vibration). Lower k allows visible vibration and bending.

Tip
