Water2 — H2O with Lone Pairs

Each molecule is an oxygen atom, two hydrogens, and two lone electron pairs arranged on a tetrahedron around oxygen. Lone-pair sites are placed closer to oxygen than hydrogens to avoid over-extended electron lobes. Hydrogen bonding is directional: donor H points toward an acceptor lone-pair site.
