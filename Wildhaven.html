Welcome to Wildhaven

Let a little wild in.

A solo sanctuary game about British mammals. You have four seasons, six turns each. Reach your chosen points target by the end of winter.

1. Welcome wildlife

Choose an arrival on the right. Pay its care cost and it settles in its named habitat. Each species may settle once.

2. Restore connections

Each habitat starts with two spaces. Restore it for 4 care, then 6 care, to open one more space and earn 3 points each time.

3. Build your rhythm

Tending gains 3 care plus 1 per forager. Watching earns 1 point per resident plus 1 per populated habitat and 1 per watcher.

4. Follow the seasons

Each seasonal goal pays up to 6 points. Connected habitats earn care at season end. At the finish, diversity and sleepy residents add bonuses.

Creature talents: ❧ Foragers add care when tending. ◎ Watchers add points when watching. ♧ Sleepers earn 3 extra points at the end of winter. All residents earn their printed points on arrival.

There is no feeding bill and residents stay all year. Care represents habitat work, not feeding wild mammals. Habitats and abilities are simplified for play. Optional challenge targets change the goal, not the wildlife.

Nature notes

Otters use waterside habitats, hazel dormice rely on connected woodland and hedgerows and hedgehogs forage along woodland edges. Learn more from the Woodland Trust. Encounter chances are game balance choices, not population estimates. Restoration in this game is a symbolic action.
