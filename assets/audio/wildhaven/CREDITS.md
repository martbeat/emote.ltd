# Wildhaven wildlife recordings

These recordings retain the licences below. The game code and original track diagrams are separate works. Source descriptions and licences were checked on 5 November 2026.

Each local MP3 is a six-second excerpt converted to mono 44.1 kHz / 96 kbps, adjusted in loudness and given short fades. No animal sounds were synthesised. Playback is user initiated, one recording at a time, with a remembered mute setting.

## Track diagrams and clues

Original SVG diagrams are simplified teaching schematics, not tracings of source photographs and not to scale. They should not be used as definitive identification keys. Mud, gait and overlap can hide toes or claw marks.

Factual references:

- [The Wildlife Trusts: Identify tracks](https://www.wildlifetrusts.org/how-identify/identify-tracks): badger, otter, hedgehog and water vole patterns.
- [Woodland Trust: Animal tracks](https://www.woodlandtrust.org.uk/blog/animal-tracks-snow-print-identification/): fox and bounding rabbit/hare comparisons.
- [Bat Conservation Trust: Soprano pipistrelle](https://www.bats.org.uk/about-bats/what-are-bats/uk-bats/soprano-pipistrelle): heterodyne detector interpretation around 55 kHz.

Watching settled wildlife records one new supported sign at a time. Notebook entries have no score effect and are reversed with the watch when using Undo. Existing saves can continue without a reset.
