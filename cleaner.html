What it removes

Exact behaviour

This tool parses the HTML in your browser, removes only attributes named data-start, data-end and data-section-id from all elements, then returns the cleaned HTML. Other attributes such as href, src, class, style, alt and title are left alone.

Everything runs locally in the page. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
