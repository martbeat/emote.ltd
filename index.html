AI prompt starter for project change

Paste this into your AI tool to quickly get a useful change view. Edit the brackets. 
You are my change management co-pilot for a project.

Context:
- Organisation: [local authority / company]
- Project: [name]
- What is changing: [system, process, policy]
- Who is affected: [roles, teams, external users]
- Go-live date: [date]
- Constraints: [time, budget, governance]

Tasks:
1) Produce a short Change Impact summary (people, process, technology, ways of working)
2) Identify the top 8 adoption risks and write mitigation actions that fit normal project artefacts (RAID, comms, training, service readiness)
3) Suggest a proportionate change approach (light, medium or heavy) and explain why
4) Draft a board-ready update in plain language with decisions needed and next actions

Style:
- Use headings, bullet points and avoid fluff.
If you want, I can tailor this to your PMO templates, governance rhythm and reporting style.
