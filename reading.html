Reading and influences

I am often asked what I read, follow, or return to when I am thinking about change, leadership, and the role of technology.

This page is not a list of recommended resources. It is a map of influence. Some of these sources are comfortable. Others are deliberately unsettling. All of them have shaped how I think about organisations as systems, how learning really happens, and why good intentions are rarely enough.
