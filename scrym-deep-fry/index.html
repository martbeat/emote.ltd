The cardboard engineering department

Build the Box

A printable A4 cut-out template for your own desk-sized version of the legendary 100% cardboard kick bass. No roadie required.

Print · stick · cut · fold

Mini Kick Bass Kit

Print at 100% / Actual Size, stick the sheet to thin recycled cardboard, cut the solid red outline, score the dashed blue folds and glue the tabs. Finished model is about 60 × 60 × 30 mm.

  1. Print the A4 sheet at 100%. Do not use “fit to page”.
  2. Glue it to cereal-box card or similarly thin cardboard and allow it to dry.
  3. Cut around the solid red line, then score and fold the dashed blue lines.
  4. Glue the long side tab first, then close the top and bottom.
