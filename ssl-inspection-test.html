SSL Inspection Test

This page cannot directly read your certificate chain because browsers block that. It checks for practical signs of enterprise proxying, SSL inspection or browser management.

Manual certificate check

In Chrome or Edge, click the icon next to the address bar, then Connection is secure, then Certificate is valid.

If the issuer says your employer, council, Zscaler, Forcepoint, Netskope, Sophos, Fortinet, Palo Alto or similar, SSL inspection is likely.
