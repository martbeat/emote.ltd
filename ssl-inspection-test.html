SSL Inspection Test

This page cannot directly read your certificate chain because browsers block that. It checks for practical signs of enterprise proxying, SSL inspection or browser management.

Manual certificate check

In Chrome or Edge, click the icon next to the address bar, then Connection is secure, then Certificate is valid.

If the issuer says your employer, council, Zscaler, Forcepoint, Netskope, Sophos, Fortinet, Palo Alto or similar, SSL inspection is likely.

What would suggest SSL inspection?

The strongest sign is that the certificate issuer changes when you move from mobile data to a work device, work VPN or work Wi-Fi.

What you seeWhat it means
Google Trust Services / WE1 Likely normal for this site.
Your employer, council, Zscaler, Forcepoint, Netskope, Sophos, Fortinet, Palo Alto, Cisco Umbrella or another internal certificate authority Strong evidence of SSL inspection.
A different certificate fingerprint but the same public issuer May be normal certificate rotation. Check the issuer first.
